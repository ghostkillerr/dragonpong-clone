# MIT KEMAR HRTF data

`assets/mit-kemar-compact.zip` contains the KEMAR head related impulse response
measurements by **Bill Gardner and Keith Martin**, MIT Media Lab, 1994.

Official source: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html

The MIT Media Lab provides the data free with no restrictions on use, provided
the authors are cited when the data is used in research or commercial work.
The original 44.1 kHz, 128 sample stereo WAV responses remain in the ZIP.
The runtime resamples each response to 48 kHz in memory.
